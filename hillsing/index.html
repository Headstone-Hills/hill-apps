Voice controls

Play without a MIDI keyboard

Computer keys: ASDFGHJKL are white keys, WETYUOP are black keys. Z/X change octave (). Hold one key and press another for legato. Left / Right arrow keys step back and forward by syllable (Alt/Option + arrow jumps a word, Home goes to the start) when the text box is not focused. On a phone, use the on-screen piano.

MIDI navigation

Bind a spare key, pad or CC button: click Learn, then press it. Bound notes are not sung.

Mod wheel = vibrato, pitch wheel = bend (±2 semitones), sustain pedal holds the vowel.
