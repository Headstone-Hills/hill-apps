UTAU voicebank

Pick a UTAU voicebank folder (the one with oto.ini), or a .zip of it (the way to load one on a phone). The files are read in place; nothing is uploaded or copied. Voicebanks are never included with HillSing.

Effects apply to every voice, in this order: bitcrush, distortion, EQ, compressor, delay, reverb

Voice controls

Play without a MIDI keyboard

Computer keys: ASDFGHJKL are white keys, WETYUOP are black keys. Z/X change octave (). Hold one key and press another for legato. Arrow keys / Home navigate the lyrics when the text box is not focused.

MIDI navigation

Bind a spare key, pad or CC button: click Learn, then press it. Bound notes are not sung.

Mod wheel = vibrato, pitch wheel = bend (±2 semitones), sustain pedal holds the vowel.
